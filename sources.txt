Basic GLSL vertex shader


A vector in GLSL has a maximum size of 4 and each of its values 
can be retrieved via vec.x, vec.y, vec.z and vec.w respectively 
where each of them represents a coordinate in space. Note that 
the vec.w component is not used as a position in space (we're dealing 
with 3D, not 4D) but is used for something called perspective division
